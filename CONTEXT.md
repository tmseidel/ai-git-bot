# Review domain

## Language

**ReviewMemory**: the bounded, revision-bound review or clarification ledger. A draft is diagnostic; insufficient context never authorizes publication.

**RevisionKey**: the exact head commit, base commit, and hash of the full canonical diff. All three identify the reviewed revision.

**SHADOW**: a permanently nonpublishing review outcome, not a successful published review and never a clarification baseline.

**PublicationCommand**: the immutable authorization to publish one exact, validated review result under its frozen revision and policy. It is not model-authored prose or permission to regenerate a result.
